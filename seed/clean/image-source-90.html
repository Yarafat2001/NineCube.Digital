    Description

    Maintain Your Car Care Routine with the Complete Car Care Kit

    The Turtle Wax Complete Car Care Kit is the ultimate solution for maintaining a pristine vehicle, inside and out. This all-inclusive kit features premium products designed to provide high-quality care for your car’s interior, exterior, and tires, ensuring lasting shine and protection.

    • Hybrid Solutions Hyper Foam: A deep-cleaning, high-foaming wheel cleaner that tackles stubborn dirt, brake dust, and grime, leaving wheels and tires spotless. The powerful foam action clings to surfaces, breaking down tough contaminants without damaging your car's finish.
    • Hybrid Solutions Ceramic Wash & Wax: This 2-in-1 solution cleans and enhances shine in one easy step. Infused with ceramic technology, it not only washes but also adds hydrophobic protection to your car’s paint, making water and contaminants easier to wash away.
    • Hybrid Solutions Ceramic Polish & Wax: Smooths out surface imperfections, while delivering a mirror-like finish. The formula also provides long-lasting ceramic protection to help preserve your car’s shine and guard against the elements.
    • Hybrid Solutions Ceramic Spray Coating: This easy-to-apply spray creates a glossy, durable ceramic layer that seals in protection for months. It’s a quick and effective way to enhance your car’s shine while providing long-term hydrophobic protection.
    • Hybrid Solutions Graphene Acrylic Tire Shine: Offers a sleek, wet-look finish with a graphene-infused formula that adds extra durability. Protects tires from cracking, fading, and dirt, keeping them looking fresh while providing superior water resistance.
    • Hybrid Solutions Inside Job: The ultimate interior cleaner, designed to tackle a variety of surfaces like vinyl, plastic, and rubber. It leaves streak-free results and neutralizes odors, ensuring your car’s cabin remains fresh and clean.

    This comprehensive kit provides everything you need for regular maintenance and ensures your vehicle stays protected and looking its best both inside and out.

    How To Use

    1. Park your car in a shaded area to prevent water spots and product drying too quickly.
    2. Spray Hybrid Solutions Hyper Foam Wheel Cleaner & Tire Prep generously on wheels and tires. Allow the foam to dwell for 30 seconds to lift dirt and grime. Use the Wheel & Tire Brush to scrub thoroughly, focusing on intricate areas. Rinse with water and repeat if necessary.
    3. Fill a bucket with water and add Hybrid Solutions Wash & Wax as per instructions. Use the pressure washer or hose to rinse the car thoroughly. Use the Plush Microfiber Wash Mitt to gently clean the car from top to bottom, rinsing frequently. Rinse thoroughly to remove all soap residue.
    4. Spray Hybrid Solutions Ceramic Wax Spray directly onto the paint surface or a microfiber towel. Spread evenly with light pressure, then buff to a high-gloss shine with a Black Microfiber Towel. For additional layers of protection, reapply after the first coat cures for 24 hours.
    5. Spray Hybrid Solutions Graphene Acrylic Tire Shine Spray Coating onto a tire applicator or directly on the tires. Spread evenly to achieve a rich, blackened finish.
    6. Spray Hybrid Solutions Ceramic + Graphene Inside Job onto interior surfaces like dashboards and door panels. Wipe clean with a Black Microfiber Towel to leave a protected, dust-resistant finish.
