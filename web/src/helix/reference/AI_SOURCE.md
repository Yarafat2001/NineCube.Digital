## Implementation guidance

When reproducing or adapting this project:

- use the existing source as the reference implementation;
- preserve the established motion and geometry unless a requested change requires otherwise;
- keep card order and asset mapping intact unless content is intentionally changed;
- preserve the relationship between the helix, guide lines, typography, grid transition, and footer blend;
- confirm the finished result against `VALIDATION.md`.
